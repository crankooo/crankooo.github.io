Namira Haris is a multimedia journalist and interactive storyteller who just graduated with her Master of Science in Media Innovation and Data Communication from Northeastern University (Class of 2026). She is Senior Editor at Storybench, Northeastern's media innovation publication.

Her work explores social equity, technology, and environmental impact — translated into investigative web projects, documentary films, audio investigations, and data-driven visual narratives. She tracks live subway delays, exposes fashion return logistics, investigates aging dams, and examines how AI reshapes journalism.
